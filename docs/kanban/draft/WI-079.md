## 1. Objective and invariant
Compute count of failed prior swing signals on the same ticker in the last 20 bars; a failure is counted only after its outcome resolves (lag >= h), highest leakage risk in the family.
- Invariants: n_jobs=2, float32 cast immediately after calc, symbol-sequential / pyarrow.dataset reads, no all-ticker uncompressed frame. Predictive-only license (D-023): no trading/economic metrics in DoD.
- Prereg: this card is the prereg for its own BH-FDR family; family id `l0-failcount`; eval job is sized separately and checkpointed.

## 2. I/O contract and blueprint (literal)
- Input: `data/raw_ohlcv/market=<MKT>/symbol=<T>.parquet` (date date32, open/high/low/close/volume float, average float, barCount int64).
- Output: `data/features/l0_ext/fail_count_{MKT}.parquet` — cols `symbol str, date date32, fail_count_20 int16` float32.
- Dry-run subset: first 10 symbols per market; smoke `--out /tmp/WI-079_smoke` (never overwrites frozen report names).
- `fail_count.py`: function `compute_fail_count(lf, window=20) -> lf`; strictly causal (bar t close and earlier only); NaN-propagating windows; index reset before concat (RISKS R-08).
- `tests/l0_ext/test_fail_count.py`: synthetic multi-ticker OHLCV fixture; causality (future-bar perturbation invariance), dtype float32, window edges NaN.
