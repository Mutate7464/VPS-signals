## 1. Objective and invariant
Compute confirmed prior swing high/low and active 50%/75% retracement/extension distance features, known as of bar t (confirmation-based, per WI-080 boundary).
- Invariants: n_jobs=2, float32 cast immediately after calc, symbol-sequential / pyarrow.dataset reads, no all-ticker uncompressed frame. Predictive-only license (D-023): no trading/economic metrics in DoD.

- Output: `data/features/l0_ext/swing_levels_{MKT}.parquet` — cols `symbol str, date date32, dist_to_swing_hi_atr float32, dist_to_swing_lo_atr float32, dist_ret50_atr float32, dist_ret75_atr float32` float32.
