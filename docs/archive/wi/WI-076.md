## 1. Objective and invariant
Compute CLV = ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low) per bar, plus a 14-bar rolling mean; NaN when high==low.
- Invariants: n_jobs=2, float32 cast immediately after calc, symbol-sequential / pyarrow.dataset reads, no all-ticker uncompressed frame. Predictive-only license (D-023): no trading/economic metrics in DoD.
- Prereg: this card is the prereg for its own BH-FDR family; family id `l0-clv`; eval job is sized separately and checkpointed.
