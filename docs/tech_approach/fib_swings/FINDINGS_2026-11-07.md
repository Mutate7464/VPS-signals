- Outputs: `regime_daily_soft.csv`, `GMM_STATES.md`. Code: `scripts/fib_regime_unsupervised.py` (initial, 4 synthetic tests).

### WI-028 — Entry-window study: INCONCLUSIVE → at-bottom dominates
- All-bottoms ensemble under first-touch barrier sim at k∈{0,3,5} (confirmed-delay entries). Full universe (855k event×policy×k rows).
- **k=0 (at-bottom):** 5/5 fold-stable, mean +0.73%/trade, full volume.
- **k=3 (confirmed):** 5/5 fold-stable, 33% volume, mean +0.27%/trade — worse on every metric.
- **k=5:** 4/5 fold-stable, 28% volume, mean +0.17%/trade.
- No regime where delayed entry beats at-bottom. Confirmation reduces volume and edge with no stability gain. **At-bottom entry is the live answer.**
- Outputs: `entry_window_labels.parquet`, `entry_window_summary.csv`, `ENTRY_WINDOW.md`. Code: `scripts/fib_entry_window.py`, 4 synthetic tests.
