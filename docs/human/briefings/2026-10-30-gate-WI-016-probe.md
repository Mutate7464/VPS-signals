# Gate Brief — WI-016 probe-first gate (WI-016, traceable to G-001)

**Verdict in one line:** The triple-barrier fib-ladder labels clear the probe gate for **CAD** (|IC| ≈ 0.045 > cost), but **not for USD** (best |IC| ≈ 0.018 < the 20 bps cost overlay), so only CAD is eligible to proceed to step 6.
**Decision requested:** APPROVE to continue with CAD only (start step 6) | HOLD | REJECT

## What was supposed to happen

Build the triple-barrier fib labels + unified fib feature grid, then run a **probe-first gate**: compute within-date rank-IC of the unified features and the E5 factors against the barrier outcomes, control multiple testing with BH-FDR, add a 5-seed placebo and the WI-009b same-sign clause, and apply a cost overlay with a NO_TRADE default. No model is trained and no order is emitted here — this gate decides whether the conditional meta-labeler (step 6) may run, and for which market.

## What actually happened

All five dispatched steps executed and all 52 new tests pass. The probe ran on the full panels (`reports/wi016_probe_CAD.md` = 833,859 rows / 116 features / 10 targets / 1,160 tests; `reports/wi016_probe_USD.md` = 1,459,630 rows). BH-FDR finds many significant, same-sign, placebo-clear features in both markets, but the **cost overlay** separates them: CAD's best test (`yangzhang_21` vs `tb_label_k20_h8`, IC −0.0481, t −14.41, q 0.0000) yields ≈ 48 bps gross vs 20 bps cost → tradeable; USD's best (IC −0.0179) yields ≈ 18 bps gross → NO_TRADE for every feature. The label comparison (`reports/wi016_legacy_comparison_{USD,CAD}.md`) ran legacy five labels vs triple-barrier on identical rows/features; the strong E5-family predictors are short-side vol/range features.

## Risks & what could be wrong

- The cost overlay is a **proxy** (|IC|·1000 bps); if the real WI-011 cost model is materially lower, USD could become viable — worth checking before writing it off.
- Long-horizon overlap (h = 144/233) inflates t-stats (registered in RISKS.md); the same-sign + placebo clauses mitigate but do not remove this.
- CAD's edge is negative-IC (short-side vol/range): a short-only or short-tilted book is implied — confirm that is acceptable before step 6.
- The 5-seed placebo is applied only to the top-100 FDR survivors (host-time bound), documented in the report.

## One thing worth learning

**The cost overlay is the real gate.** Multiple-testing control (BH-FDR) answers "is this IC real?", placebo answers "could chance produce it?", and the same-sign clause answers "is the sign stable?" — but none of those asks "is the edge bigger than the cost of trading it?". USD has a statistically real but economically too-small signal; a high-recall/low-precision primary plus a tiny edge is exactly what the NO_TRADE default is designed to reject.
